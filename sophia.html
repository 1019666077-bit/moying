"Sophia" drawn in the Regular (楷书) brush style. Open it in the generator to switch styles, paper and ink.

How Sophia is written

Moying draws each letter of Sophia with a brush font made for Chinese calligraphy, so the name keeps its spelling but gains the weight, stroke and ink-on-paper texture of traditional writing. Five styles are available — Neat, Regular, Flowing, Grass and Wild — along with horizontal or vertical layout.

The Chinese characters for Sophia

As a transliteration, Sophia is usually written 索菲娅 (Suǒfēiyà). These characters are chosen for their sound, not their meaning. Moying does not translate names — it draws the letters you type, so you can also type 索菲娅 directly into the generator to see those characters in brush.
