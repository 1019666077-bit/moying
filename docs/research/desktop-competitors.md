# Desktop companion competitors (Little Plot)

Retrieved 26 Sep 2026 from the public Steam store API (`appdetails`, `storesearch`) and review API (`appreviews`). Counts change every day. Sale prices below are the US price at retrieval. List price is the one before the current discount.

**Owner estimates are not Steam owner counts.** For paid games only, copies ≈ review count × 30 to × 50. That band sits inside the range VG Insights published in 2021 for games released in 2020 or later (20–55×, with 30× as their midpoint). Source: [Game Developer, 2021](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/how-to-estimate-steam-video-game-sales-in-2021-). Do not use it on free games. Discounts, regional prices, and refunds are not in the band. Rusty's developer told GameDiscoverCo that about 330,000 copies had sold by August 2024, when the review count was lower than today's 14,866, so ×30 on today's reviews (about 446,000) is already higher than that older disclosure. Treat every range as a rough scale.

## 给主人看的结论

检索日 2026-09-26。下面的份数是估算，不是 Steam 公布的拥有人数。只对买断制游戏使用：评测条数 × 30 到 × 50。免费游戏不算进这个乘法。

**1. 挤不挤。** 「贴在屏幕边上的挂机」已经很挤。Steam 有一整包名叫 Bottom-Of-Your-Screen 的捆绑，里面是底栏农场、钓鱼、猫咖。免费桌宠更大：虚拟桌宠 VPet 有 51,847 条评测，其中简体中文 43,302 条。Bongo Cat 免费，111,261 条评测。你要的「一小块俯视墓地，骷髅、僵尸、鬼魂、三头狗一起走」没有同款。挤的是边栏挂机，不是这块墓地。

**2. 最近的五个。**

- Little Ghosties：桌面玻璃箱里养小鬼。2026-09-07 上市，检索时标价 $4.99（9 月 12 日另一站记过 $3.99，价格会变）。59 条评测，93.2% 好评。商店语言列表里没有简体中文。
- Petunia's Purgatory：屏幕底栏的阴森农场，有血和旧神，是侧面不是俯视。2026-07-16，$6.99。88 条评测，95.5% 好评。另有 $3.99 装饰向支持包。
- Tiny Pasture：底栏小牧场。介绍文案里后继动物包含幽灵和僵尸，但是侧面牧场，不是坟场。2025-02-17，标价 $5.99。8,629 条评测，92.5% 好评。发行方是 Gamersky Games。简体评测 5,815 条，约占全部评测的 67%。
- Gothic Kitty Cat Idler：坟场门口抽猫，不是四只怪在地上走。商店写计划 2026-10-30，还没有评测，标价页上还没有。
- Mortis（itch，不在 Steam）：一只像素骷髅贴着屏幕走，点击能穿透。页面写明画是 PixelLab 生成的。不是一小块共用的地，也没有僵尸、鬼魂和三头狗。

**3. 差评里反复出现、我们应该避开的。** 这些是抽到的英文或中文差评原意，不是全部玩家的统计。

- 存档丢失，或挂着挂着崩溃。Desktop Purrfect、Nanomon、Cornerpond、Emberlands 的差评样本都提到。
- 名字叫挂机，其实要一直手点，一离开就停。Tiny Pasture、Rusty、Petunia 的差评都在说这个。Petunia 的中文差评还说早期没有自动种植，后来版本才加上。
- 宠物会饿死，或者隔几分钟就要喂。Nanomon 差评希望能关掉死亡。Little Ghosties 的一条差评写鬼魂大约五分钟吃一次，太吵。
- 窗口挡事、不好关。VPet 有差评问怎么退出。Little Ghosties 有差评说点进游戏后看不到后面的窗口。
- 想要的角色得另外花大钱。Desktop Mate 本体免费，差评写不花钱几乎不能换角色。角色包标价 $14.99，全语言评测只有 67.1% 好评。

**4. 值得照着做的。**

- 窗口能拖、能改大小，并写清楚可以放到别的窗口上面。Cornerpond 的好评专门夸了缩放和「盖在别的标签上」。
- 看着可爱，点一下有反应，就愿意开着。Bit Buddy（$1.99，549 条，99.3% 好评）和 Little Ghosties 的好评都是这个语气。
- 一次买断。装饰用便宜小包，不要 $15 一个角色。Burgie's Cozy Kitchen 的万圣节装饰包标价 $2.99。Tiny Pasture 的支持包标价 $1.99，另有一个 $2.99 的水域包和两个免费动物包。
- 简体中文第一天就做。VPet 约 84% 的评测是简体，而且是「好评如潮」。Tiny Pasture 约 67%。Rusty 约 25%（3,771 / 14,866），简体评测同样是「好评如潮」。反例：Cornerpond 语言列表里没有简体，仍然有 2,745 条评测、97.1% 好评。所以中文是加分，不是销量保证。Task Bar Hero 的简体评测是「多半差评」（9,831 条里只有 2,417 条好评）。中文玩家会直接写「这不是挂机」。

**5. 建议。** 标价 $4.99，一次买断，四只怪都在里面。窗口用一小块能拖走的俯视墓地。不要做成细底栏（俯视需要一块地），也不要让它们满屏幕跑（itch 上的单只骷髅已经这么做了，而且会挡点击）。最先做的四件事：能拖、能缩小；存档不会丢；点一下会动，打字只在本机让它们走得快一点，不会饿死、不会每五分钟讨食；界面有简体中文。

**6. 做不做。** 可以做，当成小试验，不要当成全年主业。计划时用 Little Ghosties 和 Petunia 的量级，不要用 Tiny Pasture 或 Rusty。Little Ghosties 59 条评测 × 30 到 × 50，大约 1,800–3,000 份。Petunia 88 条，大约 2,600–4,400 份。若几乎没有短视频，按几十条评测来想，也就是大约一千到几千份，还要再扣平台分成和打折。Rusty 将近 33 万份是作者在 2024 年 8 月说的，当时已有约 14 万愿望单、短视频，并且赶上了 Steam 的农耕节。那不是默许的结果。

## Method and window words

- **Bottom strip:** the store text says it sits at the bottom of the screen. These are side-view scenes, not top-down plots.
- **Corner / anywhere:** a small toy you place, still not described as a top-down graveyard.
- **Roam:** the pet walks across the desktop or sits on window edges.
- **Taskbar:** lives in or punches the taskbar.
- **Room:** a focus or chill scene, not a desktop pet.
- **zh:** Simplified Chinese is in the store's supported-language list. This is not the same as "reviews written in Chinese."

Praise and complaint cells are one sampled review each, shortened. They are not a count of how often the complaint appears.

## Microsoft Store

Official individual registration: the new flow has **no registration fee**. The old one-time fee was $19, and it still appears if you enter through Partner Center, Xbox, or Visual Studio instead of [storedeveloper.microsoft.com](https://storedeveloper.microsoft.com). Sources: [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/publish/whats-new-individual-developer), [Windows Developer Blog, 10 Sep 2025](https://blogs.windows.com/windowsdeveloper/2025/09/10/free-developer-registration-for-individual-developers-on-microsoft-store/). Identity check is a government ID and a selfie.

Code signing: if you ship an **MSIX** through the Store, Microsoft re-signs it and Store installs are not subject to SmartScreen download warnings. If you ship an **MSI or EXE** through the Store, you must Authenticode-sign it yourself, and SmartScreen can still warn until the file has a reputation. Sources: [code signing options](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/package-and-deploy/code-signing-options), [SmartScreen reputation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/package-and-deploy/smartscreen-reputation).

Visibility: this pass did not find a Store chart of desktop pets. The one concrete listing is second-hand. Pets Therapy's own site says the app is free on the Microsoft Store (and that 37 pets stay free). The Steam listing of the same name is $9.99 with 3 reviews. Source: [pets-therapy.com](https://pets-therapy.com/desktop-tamagotchi.html). Treat the Store as a later, low-discovery shelf, not the launch store. Steam is where the review counts above actually are.

## Direct overlap

Nothing found does all of this at once: top-down view, one small always-on-top graveyard, skeleton + zombie + ghost + three-headed dog, one-time price around $5.

The five nearest, and the gap:

1. **Little Ghosties** already owns "cute ghosts in a box on the desktop," at the same $4.99, with feeding and collecting. It is not top-down, has no skeleton, zombie, or three-headed dog, and has no Simplified Chinese. Complaints say the box is thin and the feeding nags.
2. **Petunia's Purgatory** owns "creepy-cute desktop idle" but as a side-view bottom farm with blood and an elder god. Heavier than cute-spooky, and players asked for less mandatory clicking.
3. **Tiny Pasture** puts ghosts and zombies in a bottom-strip pasture as animals among others, published by Gamersky, with thousands of reviews. It is not a graveyard and not top-down. Do not imply those creatures are yours alone.
4. **Gothic Kitty Cat Idler** uses a graveyard and a Halloween date (30 Oct 2026) but the toy is a cat summon every minute. Unreleased, no price, no reviews.
5. **Mortis** is the literal desktop skeleton, free-standing on itch, click-through, one character, AI-assisted pixels disclosed on the page. A paid little plot with four creatures and a shared ground is a different product. Mochi is the same shape for a ghost.

## How small games actually got seen

Documented cases, not a formula:

- **Rusty's Retirement.** Jordan Morris told GameDiscoverCo (28 Aug 2024) the game launched with about 140,000 wishlists, sold almost 330,000 copies in about four months, and that he posted Instagram Reels and cross-posted them to TikTok. He launched on 26 Apr 2024 just before Steam's Farming Fest on 29 Apr 2024. He already had players from Haiku the Robot. A $3.99-style supporter skin did about 36,000 units by that article (he called it a $4 golden outfit). Source: [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-rustys-retirement-idle-farmed).
- **Bongo Cat.** Marcel Zurawka told Polygon and Eurogamer the toy came from Rusty's "sits on the screen" idea plus the Banana marketplace meme, using the existing Bongo Cat meme. Most of the small revenue was cheap cosmetic skins, not player-to-player trades. Sources: [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/pc/561909/bongo-cat-viral-clicker-game-steam/), [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/steams-fourth-most-played-game-doesnt-really-make-any-money-and-its-developer-says-its-better-off-that-way).
- **Little Ghosties.** The developer wrote that Early Access launched with 10,000 Steam wishlists. Quoted on [SteamRaw](https://steamraw.com/app/4132960/little-ghosties/). That is a wishlist count, not copies sold.
- **Tiny Pasture.** Store page publisher is Gamersky Games, and 67% of reviews are Simplified Chinese. That is distribution evidence, not a counted ad spend. No separate TikTok view count was retrieved.
- **Press lists, not the dev's own posts.** [Pixelkin, 6 Jun 2025](https://pixelkin.org/2025/06/06/8-best-desktop-idlers-like-rustys-retirement-to-sit-on-your-screen/) and [Maybe Sarisa, 26 Jul 2026](https://maybesarisa.com/cozy-idle-games-list-for-the-bottom-of-your-screen/) round up the bottom-of-screen genre. Useful later, once a build exists. They are not something you can buy.

No retrieved source shows a faceless devlog alone producing Rusty-scale wishlists. The documented spikes had a prior audience, a meme, a Steam themed festival, or a Chinese publisher.

## Price and DLC that exist

Bands that line up with review scores, not with revenue:

- **$1.99–$3.99** and a high positive rate: Bit Buddy $1.99 (99.3%, 549 reviews), Cornerpond $2.99 (97.1%, 2,745), Ropuka $3.99 (95.2%, 4,311), Bao Bao $3.99 (84.1%, 440). Milly at $1.99 is only 73.5% and players called it too short.
- **$4.99–$6.99** for a thicker toy: Little Ghosties $4.99, Tiny Pasture and Rusty and Petunia at $5.99–$6.99. This is the band for Little Plot.
- **$8.99–$14.99** on a thin desktop toy draws "not worth it": Desktop Cat Cafe list $9.99 (77.9%, 95 reviews), Nanomon $14.99 (150 reviews), Pets Therapy $9.99 (3 reviews).
- **Cosmetic packs that are on the store, unit sales mostly unknown:** Burgie Halloween cosmetics $2.99 (released 31 Oct 2025) and a dungeon cosmetics pack $2.99. Tiny Pasture supporter pack $1.99, Azure Waters $2.99, two animal packs free. Petunia Devoted Supporter Pack $3.99. Spirit City room DLC $7.99 (Magical Library, Enchanted Garden). Bongo hat packs about $5–$10. Desktop Mate character DLC $14.99, and the base app's reviews are Mixed.

Copy the $2–$4 cosmetic, not the $15 character. Put the four creatures in the $4.99. A later tombstone or hat pack can follow Burgie's $2.99 Halloween pack. Do not gate the skeleton, zombie, ghost, or dog behind DLC.

## What this means for Little Plot
