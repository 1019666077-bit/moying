The Chinese characters for Summer

As a transliteration, Summer is usually written 萨默 (Sà mò). These characters are chosen for their sound, not their meaning, and are given here for reference — Moying does not translate names. Please have a person review the characters before any permanent use such as a tattoo.

How Summer is written

Moying draws each letter of Summer with a brush font made for Chinese calligraphy, so the name keeps its spelling but gains the weight, stroke and ink-on-paper texture of traditional writing.
