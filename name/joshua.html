The Chinese characters for Joshua

As a transliteration, Joshua is usually written 乔舒亚 (Qiáo shū yà). These characters are chosen for their sound, not their meaning, and are given here for reference — Moying does not translate names. Please have a person review the characters before any permanent use such as a tattoo.

How Joshua is written
