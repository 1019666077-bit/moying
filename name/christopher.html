The Chinese characters for Christopher

As a transliteration, Christopher is usually written 克里斯托弗 (Kè lǐ sī tuō fú). These characters are chosen for their sound, not their meaning, and are given here for reference — Moying does not translate names. Please have a person review the characters before any permanent use such as a tattoo.
