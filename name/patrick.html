The Chinese characters for Patrick

As a transliteration, Patrick is usually written 帕特里克 (Pà tè lǐ kè). These characters are chosen for their sound, not their meaning, and are given here for reference — Moying does not translate names. Please have a person review the characters before any permanent use such as a tattoo.

How Patrick is written
