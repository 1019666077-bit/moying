The Chinese characters for Thomas

As a transliteration, Thomas is usually written 托马斯 (Tuō mǎ sī). These characters are chosen for their sound, not their meaning, and are given here for reference — Moying does not translate names. Please have a person review the characters before any permanent use such as a tattoo.
