How it works

Moying draws each letter of your word with brush fonts created for Chinese calligraphy, so your name keeps its spelling but gains the weight, stroke and ink-on-paper texture of traditional writing. It does not translate your name — it writes it.

Five brush styles

Choose the script that fits your design: Neat for a tidy look, Regular (楷书) for a classic calligraphy feel, Flowing (行书) for a lighter hand, Grass (草书) for speed and energy, and Wild for an expressive, uneven stroke. You can also pick horizontal or vertical layout, rice, aged or night paper, and black ink or red cinnabar.
