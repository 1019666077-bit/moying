How Olivia is written

Moying draws each letter of Olivia with a brush font made for Chinese calligraphy, so the name keeps its spelling but gains the weight, stroke and ink-on-paper texture of traditional writing. Five styles are available — Neat, Regular, Flowing, Grass and Wild — along with horizontal or vertical layout.

The Chinese characters for Olivia

As a transliteration, Olivia is usually written 奥利维亚 (Àolìwéiyà). These characters are chosen for their sound, not their meaning, and they are given here for reference — Moying does not translate names. The generator draws the English letters you type, so enter "Olivia" to see the name in brush.
