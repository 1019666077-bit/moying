The Chinese characters for dream

There are two useful forms. 梦 (mèng) means a dream in the sense of something dreamed while asleep, while 梦想 (mèngxiǎng) means a dream or aspiration — the goal you are working toward. For a tattoo or an encouragement gift, 梦想 captures the "follow your dreams" meaning.
