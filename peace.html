The Chinese characters for peace

Two characters carry the idea of peace. 和平 (hépíng) means peace in the sense of no war or conflict. 安 (ān) means peace, calm and safety — it appears in 平安 (píng'ān), the classic blessing of peace and well-being that suits gifts, door signs and new-year wishes.
