How it works — 3 steps

1

Install the extension

Add PhotoSweep to Chrome. It asks for only two things: local storage, and permission to run on photos.google.com.

2

Open photos.google.com

Sign in as usual and open the album, search result, or view you want to clear out. PhotoSweep works on the page you already have open.

3

Set a number and press Start

Open the popup, choose how many items to move (5, 20, 100 — your call), then press Start. You can hit Stop whenever you want.

Safety first

  • Items go to Trash, not gone forever. Everything PhotoSweep removes sits in your Google Photos Trash, where you can restore it for 60 days.
  • It runs locally in your browser. There is no server doing the deleting. The clicking happens on your own computer, in your own tab.
  • Your photos are never uploaded. PhotoSweep does not send your photos, filenames, faces, or locations anywhere.
  • Minimal permissions. Local storage plus access to photos.google.com. No analytics, no ad networks, no third-party trackers.
  • It uses the normal Google Photos buttons. The same Move to trash controls you would click yourself — not a private API.
  • You stay in control. Set a small number the first time. Watch what happens. Raise it when you are comfortable.

FAQ

Is it safe to use?

Yes. PhotoSweep clicks the same “Move to trash” controls you would click yourself, in your own signed-in tab. Nothing is sent to us, your photos are never uploaded, and the extension only asks for local storage and access to photos.google.com.

Can I undo a deletion?

Yes. PhotoSweep moves items to Trash — it does not permanently delete them. You can restore anything from Google Photos Trash for 60 days. That is why we recommend starting with a small number, like 5, on your first run.

Does it work in other languages?

PhotoSweep is tested with the English and Chinese Google Photos interface. Other languages may work but are not guaranteed yet. If it does not work in your language, email us and we will look into it.

How do I activate Pro?

After you buy, you receive a license key. Open the PhotoSweep popup in Chrome, paste the license key into the license field, and press Activate. The extension checks the key online and caches the result on your computer, so short offline periods are fine.
