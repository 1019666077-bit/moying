Privacy Policy — PhotoSweep

Last updated: October 7, 2026

PhotoSweep is a Chrome extension that helps you bulk-move items to Trash in your own Google Photos tab.

PhotoSweep is not affiliated with Google.

Summary

We do not collect your photos, account data, or browsing history. Automation runs locally in your browser on photos.google.com using the page’s normal UI while you are signed in.

Data the extension handles

Data Where Leaves the device?
Daily deletion counts (free tier) chrome.storage.local on your computer No
License key + verification cache chrome.storage.local on your computer License key only is sent when you click Activate (see below)
Popup settings (max delete, date filter) In-memory / local UI only for the session No
Photo contents, filenames, faces, location Never read for upload or analytics No

Permissions

  • storage — save daily usage and license cache locally
  • activeTab / host permission for https://photos.google.com/* — run the content script and talk to the active Photos tab

No other sites are accessed for photo deletion. There is no analytics SDK, no ad network, and no third-party tracker in the extension.

License verification (Pro)

If you enter a license key and choose Activate, the extension sends that key to the configured license endpoint (LICENSE_API in the source). The response (valid true/false) is cached locally for offline grace (72 hours).

Until payment/licensing is fully launched, that endpoint may be a placeholder. When it goes live, only the license key (not your photos) is transmitted for verification.

The Upgrade button opens a checkout URL in a new tab; checkout is handled by the payment provider (planned: Waffo), not by uploading Photos library data.

Google Photos

PhotoSweep does not use a private Google Photos API. It clicks the same Delete / Move to trash controls you would use yourself. Your Google Account session stays between you and Google.

Children

PhotoSweep is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.
