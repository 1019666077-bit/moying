Michael in Chinese Calligraphy

See how the name Michael looks written with a traditional Chinese brush, and download a clean copy for your project.

"Michael" drawn in the Regular (楷书) brush style. Open it in the generator to switch styles, paper and ink.

How Michael is written

Moying draws each letter of Michael with a brush font made for Chinese calligraphy, so the name keeps its spelling but gains the weight, stroke and ink-on-paper texture of traditional writing. Because Michael is longer, it wraps onto two lines on the default canvas — raise the size slider or switch to vertical layout to adjust it.

The Chinese characters for Michael

As a transliteration, Michael is usually written 迈克尔 (Màikè'ěr). These characters are chosen for their sound, not their meaning. Moying does not translate names — it draws the letters you type, so you can also type 迈克尔 directly into the generator to see those characters in brush.
