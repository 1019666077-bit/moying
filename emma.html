How Emma is written

Moying draws each letter of Emma with a brush font made for Chinese calligraphy, so the name keeps its spelling but gains the weight, stroke and ink-on-paper texture of traditional writing. Five styles are available — Neat, Regular, Flowing, Grass and Wild — along with horizontal or vertical layout.

The Chinese characters for Emma

As a transliteration, Emma is usually written 艾玛 (Àimǎ). These characters are chosen for their sound, not their meaning, and they are given here for reference — Moying does not translate names. The generator draws the English letters you type, so enter "Emma" to see the name in brush.
