How to pick the right one

Match the character to the occasion and the person: 福 (fortune) suits a new year or new home, 寿 (longevity) suits an elder's birthday, 喜 (joy) suits a wedding, and 爱 (love) suits a partner. If you are unsure, ask a native speaker — some characters carry specific connotations that a dictionary will not show.
