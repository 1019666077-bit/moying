| --- | --- | --- |
| zcoolxiaowei-latin.ttf | Neat | ZCOOL XiaoWei |

| longcang-latin.ttf | Flowing | Long Cang |

| zhimangxing-latin.ttf | Wild | Zhi Mang Xing |
| seal.ttf | Seal marks 墨 and 英 | ZCOOL XiaoWei |

Subsets keep U+0020–U+007E (seal.ttf keeps U+58A8 and U+82F1 only). Licenses and copyright lines are in `OFL.txt`. Rebuild with `bash scripts/fetch-fonts.sh` (needs `pyftsubset`).
